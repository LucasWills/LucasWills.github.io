Big SMPS

Big Adjustable Switching Power Supply

2023

Background

I find myself doing a lot of higher power electronics projects, and those often require a suitable power supply. Adjustable power supplies are especially useful for testing projects. So, in this project I built a large switching power supply adjustable from 3V-80V, and able to suuply up to 1500W of power.
